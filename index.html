The useful details

A few things
before you jump in.

Where should I start? ＋

Try a photo scan or browse the marketplace. Sign in to manage your inventory and collection, prepare listings, or chat with Poko. For a bigger scanning session, open Scan Connect on your desktop and pair your phone.

Can I use Pokoin on my phone? ＋

Yes. Use Pokoin in your browser, and open the camera scanner on your phone. The web tools are available today; native App Store and Google Play listings are not available yet.

How does buyer protection work? ＋

Pokoin checkout holds the buyer's site PKN in escrow until delivery is confirmed. If the seller does not ship within 7 days, the PKN is returned from escrow. Report a problem from Orders. Read the buyer protection policy for the process and timelines.

Where are the token and network details? ＋

PokoinPoS is the network behind native PKN, with chain ID 26062026. Explore the wallet, block explorer, network health, and node documentation.

The 28 May 2026 security report documents Go-native checks; it is not a paid third-party audit. Reserve proof is available separately.
