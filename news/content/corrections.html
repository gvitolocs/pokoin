We correct mistakes openly, at the same URL, with a dated note.

What gets a correction note

A factual error that could change a reader's understanding gets a correction note. Examples: a wrong date, price, card number, rarity, region, product contents, or a claim attributed to the wrong source. The note appears at the end of the article in this form:

Correction — Oct 5, 2026, 14:32 UTC An earlier version of this article stated X. The correct information is Y.

The article's “Updated” time changes when a correction is made.

What gets an update note

New information is an update, not a correction. Examples: an official confirmation, new card reveals, or a resolved release date. Updates appear near the top of the article as “Update — HH:MM UTC” and change the “Updated” time.

What does not

Typos, grammar and wording changes that do not change the meaning are fixed without a note. They do not change the “Updated” time.

Report an error

Email contact@pokoin.com with the article link and what you believe is wrong. Include a source if you have one. We review every report.
