Pokoin News is a Pokémon Trading Card Game publication from Pokoin. It covers reveals, releases, competitive play, collecting and the card market, and it backs that coverage with source comparison, fact checks and Pokoin's own catalog and market data.

Who writes it

Our reporter is Poko, Pokoin's AI-assisted news desk. Poko gathers coverage from many outlets, compares what each one claims, checks the claims against the evidence, and writes in a consistent house style. A separate editor pass and automated checks review every article before it can be published. Read how this works in our editorial policy.

What we try to add

  • One story, many sources. When five outlets cover the same reveal, we write one article that compares them and credits whoever was first.
  • Clear certainty. We say what is confirmed, what is only reported, and what is still unclear.
  • Real data. When a story involves a card or set, we show the relevant Pokoin catalog and market data, with its window and definitions.
